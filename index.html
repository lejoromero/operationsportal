Objective

The general objective of the program is to contribute to making the administrative and technical management of public investment processes more effective, reducing the loss of social benefits caused by delays in the implementation of road, water access, and school infrastructure projects. The specific objectives are: (i) to strengthen the institutional capacity of the executing agencies responsible for planning, procurement, and supervision of public works; (ii) to expand sustainable access to drinking water and basic sanitation services for rural and dispersed communities, prioritizing territories with the highest levels of unmet basic needs; and (iii) to improve the quality and climate resilience of the social infrastructure financed under the program. The program is expected to directly benefit approximately 145,000 people, including indigenous and rural populations, and to consolidate a results based management model that can be replicated in future operations of the sector.
